Thesis research at the Chair of Space Geodesy, ETH Zürich, supervised by Prof. Benedikt Soja, Dr. Betty Heller-Kaikov and Dr. Laura Crocetti.

- Deep-learning separation of non-tidal atmospheric, oceanic and hydrological loading signals plus noise from multichannel GNSS coordinate time series.
- Closed-loop simulation framework with known ground truth, used for ablations of window length and network depth.
- Validation on real GNSS stations against independent loading products.
